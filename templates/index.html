Documentation

Sign a PDF

Upload a PDF, pick or create a signature (draw it, type your name, or upload a photo of your real signature), then drag it into position on the page. Resize it with the corner handle. Optionally add a cryptographic signature for tamper-evidence.

Uploaded signature photos are automatically cleaned up: background removed and cropped tightly, regardless of lighting or paper color. Straighten the photo yourself first if it was taken at an angle — that part can't be auto-corrected.

Organize pages

Upload a PDF to see every page as a thumbnail. Drag thumbnails to reorder them, use the rotate button to spin a page 90° at a time, and the delete button to drop a page entirely. Click "Apply & download" when you're happy with the layout.

Merge

Select two or more PDFs at once in the file picker (or Ctrl/Cmd-click multiple files). They're combined in the order they were selected.

Extract pages

Enter which pages to keep as a comma-separated list, with ranges allowed: 1,3,5-8 keeps pages 1, 3, 5, 6, 7, and 8. Page numbers are 1-based (the first page is "1").

Compress

Reduces file size. "Smallest file" downsamples images the most aggressively (visible quality loss); "Balanced" is a good default; "Higher quality" compresses less but shrinks the file less too.

Fill form

Only works on PDFs that have real fillable form fields (an AcroForm) - the kind where Adobe Acrobat or Preview shows editable boxes natively. Click "Detect form fields" first; if the PDF has none, you'll get a clear message. For a scanned/flat "form" with no real fields, use the Sign tab's drag-and-drop to stamp text wherever you need it instead.

Convert to Word

Extracts text and simple tables into an editable .docx file. This is not a pixel-perfect layout clone - complex multi-column layouts, images, and precise formatting won't carry over exactly. Best for getting editable text out of a mostly-text PDF.

Add / Remove password

Add password: sets a password required to open the PDF. Remove password: enter the current password to produce an unlocked copy. Both use standard PDF encryption.

My PDFs

Every PDF you sign is saved automatically so you can come back and download it again, or click "Sign again" to reuse it in the Sign tab without re-uploading. Delete ones you no longer need. Note: this library currently only auto-saves PDFs uploaded through the Sign tab, not the other tools.
